Know what is falling.

block types. Pickups have an extra outer outline: pink for helpful pickups, yellow for harmful Haste and Curse pickups. Helpful pickups glow green; solid hazards glow red without the extra outline. Neutral Weak blocks and inactive ghosts have grey outlines. Pictures show real block shapes and textures; sizes and rotations vary during play.

Spawn chances match gameplay exactly, use at most one decimal place, and add up to 100%.

8 HP · Recover 1 HP every 8 seconds

Easy-size arena with safe edges. Blocks vary in shape, size, sway and speed—with 10% slower ordinary base speeds and a base falling-speed limit below Hard mode’s maximum. Sonic falls at 1.5× Impossible base speed; Snail moves at one third of Easy speed. Curse and Hourglass multiply these speeds. Spawns start every 1.6 seconds and ramp up to every 0.16 seconds. Pause freezes the game and healing timer.

Hits grant 1 second of contact protection. Burning and poison still tick; repeated effects refresh rather than stack. Skull blocks ignore protection. Hearts, shields and antidotes are safe pickups. Haste blocks hurt but also boost speed. Outlined ghost blocks are harmless. Rings show gravity fields and bomb blast range.

Screen-effect key

    CUBIE CASH

    Little outfits. Big personality.

    Earn Cash by playing, then collect Cubie Customs, Turtle Customs, and Turtle Trails. Your purchases stay in this browser profile.

    How to earn Cubie Cash

    Score × game multiplier × difficulty bonus = converted score. Every 100 converted score earns 1 Cubie Cash. Partial Cash carries over between rounds. Finished rounds earn Cash once, even if you lose; Key Rush also pays when you exit. Temporary Core practice does not award Cash.

    A shell for every adventure.
